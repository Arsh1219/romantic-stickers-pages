Common questions

How do I add a pack to WhatsApp?

Open a pack and tap Add to WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens and asks you to confirm. Tap Add, and the pack appears in WhatsApp's sticker tray in every chat.

The Add to WhatsApp button does nothing.

Make sure WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business is installed and updated from Google Play, and that you have opened it at least once. Then try again. If a pack is already added, the button reads Added to WhatsApp.

Can I send stickers in other apps?

Yes. Open a sticker and tap Send sticker. Android's share sheet opens, and you can send that one sticker with any app that accepts images.

Does the app read my chats?

No. The app never reads your chats, messages or contacts. The only thing it asks WhatsApp is whether a pack is already added. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Are the people in the stickers real?

No. The sticker photos are original AI generated images of fictional people. No real people are depicted.

Do I need internet?

No. All 280 stickers are inside the app, so you can browse packs and add them without a connection. Ads and subscription checks need a connection.

How do I remove a pack from WhatsApp?

In WhatsApp, open a chat, tap the sticker icon, open the sticker settings and delete the pack.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Google account you subscribed with, open Romantic Stickers, open Settings and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel?

Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Romantic Stickers, Cancel subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged.

Can I change my ad consent choice?

Yes. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, open the app's Settings and tap Ad privacy.

Is this an official WhatsApp app?

No. Romantic Stickers is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta. WhatsApp is a trademark of its owner.
