Privacy Policy for Romantic Stickers

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Romantic Stickers (shown on Google Play as Romantic Stickers: Love Couple) is an Android app with 10 packs of romantic couple stickers that you can add to WhatsApp or share through Android. This page explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the app with package com.romanticstickers.love.couple, published on Google Play by Billionweb.

The short version

  • All stickers are inside the app. The 280 stickers ship with the app. Nothing is downloaded for them, and nothing you make or own is ever uploaded.
  • No camera, no gallery, no contacts, no microphone, no location. The app does not ask for any of these and cannot reach your photos, files or people.
  • Your chats stay private. The app never reads your chats or messages. The only thing it asks WhatsApp is whether a sticker pack is already added.
  • There is no account. You never sign in, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We use Google Firebase to count how the app is used and to receive crash reports, and Google AdMob to show ads in the free version. These receive device and usage information described below.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before ads are personalised or your data is used for advertising, and you can change that choice at any time.

How stickers reach WhatsApp

When you tap Add to WhatsApp, the app uses WhatsApp's official sticker pack add flow, and WhatsApp asks you to confirm. To let WhatsApp copy the pack, the app exposes its bundled sticker images through a content provider that only WhatsApp can read. It contains only the stickers that come with the app. The app also asks WhatsApp whether a pack is already added, so it can show the right button. It does not read your chats, messages, contacts or anything else in WhatsApp.

When you share a single sticker, Android's share sheet opens and you choose the app to send it with. Only that sticker image is passed to the app you choose.

About the sticker images

The sticker photos are original AI generated images of fictional people. No real people are depicted, and no photo of you or anyone you know is used.

Information that stays on your device

Your app settings, such as your language, are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not stored on a server, sent to us or shared with anyone. If you contact support from the app, your own email app opens and you decide what to send us.

Advertising and your consent choices

The free version shows ads. Premium shows no ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised and whether your data may be used for advertising and measurement. Until you choose, ads are not requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app's Settings under Ad privacy. Elsewhere, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings under Privacy, Ads.

Subscriptions

Premium is a weekly subscription sold through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment and tells the app whether a subscription is active. We receive no card details, no billing address and no name. You manage or cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the Google services listed above, which act on our behalf or under their own terms as described. These providers may process information outside your country under their own safeguards.

Retention

Settings remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Packs you added to WhatsApp stay in WhatsApp until you remove them there. Google Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months. Crash reports are retained by Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Advertising data follows Google's retention practices.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes everything the app stored on your device. To remove a pack from WhatsApp, open WhatsApp's sticker settings and delete it there. To ask us to delete analytics data associated with your installation, or to exercise any other privacy right you have under local law, email arshpreet@billionweb.co with the subject "Romantic Stickers data deletion". Because there is no account, we will explain what we can locate and what limited information, if any, we need. Never send passwords or identity documents.

Children

Romantic Stickers is a general audience app for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use the protections these services provide.
